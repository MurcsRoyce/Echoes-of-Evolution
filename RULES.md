## Core Game Rules

- **Starting Health**: Each player starts with **100 health**.
- **Starting Evolution Points**: Each player starts with **20 evolution points**.
- **Starting Hand Size**: On the **first turn only**, the player clicks **Start turn** then **Draw 8** to get their opening hand of 8 cards. Every turn after that, **Draw** gives 1 card (hand max 8). **You may only draw once per turn** — after you draw, the Draw button is disabled until your next turn.

## Card Play & Costs

- **Play Cost**: Each character card has a **play cost from 1–5** shown in the **top-right corner** of the card.
- **Rarity and Cost**: **Higher rarity cards have higher play costs** (for example, Tier 3 cards cost more than Tier 1 cards).
- **Spending Evolution Points**: To play a card from your hand, you must **spend evolution points equal to its play cost**.

## Turn Structure

- **Start turn**: When it’s your turn, you must click **Start turn** to begin. You gain **+2 evolution points** (capped at 20). Until you do, you can’t draw or play cards.
- **End turn**: Click **End turn** when you’re done. This passes the turn to the other player so they can take theirs.
- **Opponent’s turn**: While it’s the other player’s turn, you see “Opponent’s turn” and your hand/actions are disabled. When it’s your turn again, click **My turn** to switch back; then click **Start turn** to gain +2 EP and begin.

## Evolution

- **Evolution slots**: You have 2 slots — **Target** and **Burn**. You can put a card into a slot **from your hand or from your field** (cards already in play). Putting a card in a slot **costs evolution points equal to that card’s play cost** each time (you pay again when moving a card from the field). You can return a card from a slot to your hand (no cost).
  - **Target** (slot 1): The card whose stats will carry over to the evolved result (used for future stat inheritance).
  - **Burn** (slot 2): The sacrifice card; consumed for the evolution.
- **Evolve**: When both slots are filled, click **Evolve**. Both cards are removed and you receive **one new “Evolved Form” card** in your hand. The evolved card:
  - **Rank (rarity)** goes up **one step** from the **Target** card only: Tier 1 → Tier 2, Tier 2 → Tier 3, Tier 3 → Tier 3. The Burn card does not affect rank (e.g. Tier 1 in Target + Tier 3 in Burn → evolved is Tier 2).
  - Has a **random play cost between 2 and 6**.
  - Has **power** (and energy/health) equal to the sum of both cards.

## Win Condition

- Reduce your opponent’s **health to 0** to win. When you play a card, it deals damage equal to its **power** to the opponent’s health. The game ends when either player’s health reaches 0.
